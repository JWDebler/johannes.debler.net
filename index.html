About

I am a molecular plant pathologist and fungal genomicist based in Perth, Western Australia. My research focuses on the Ascochyta pathogens of cool-season grain legumes, particularly lentil and chickpea, where I investigate the molecular mechanisms of fungal pathogenicity through effector biology and comparative genomics.

My PhD at Curtin University's Centre for Crop and Disease Management (2021–2025) centred on effector discovery in Ascochyta lentis and Ascochyta rabiei. This work led to new tools for the research community, including a boscalid selectable marker system and new telomere-to-telomere (T2T) genome assemblies for two pathotypes of Ascochyta lentis.

Beyond the bench, I am passionate about science communication, demonstrated by winning FameLab Australia 2024, and about mentoring the next generation of researchers.
