Summary

Molecular plant pathologist and fungal genomicist with over ten years' experience at Curtin University's Centre for Crop and Disease Management, specialising in effector biology and comparative genomics of Ascochyta pathogens in cool-season grain legumes. Track record of developing new research tools, including a selectable marker system and telomere-to-telomere genome assemblies, alongside strong skills in molecular cloning, long-read sequencing, and bioinformatic analysis. Recognised science communicator (FameLab Australia 2024 National Winner) with experience mentoring colleagues and organising scientific conferences.
